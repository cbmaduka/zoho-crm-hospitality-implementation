# Project Overview

## Business Name

LuxeStay Hotels

## Industry

Hospitality and Hotel Management

## Business Description

LuxeStay Hotels is a fictional luxury hotel company that provides premium accommodation, corporate lodging, event hosting, airport pickup, restaurant reservations, and guest support services.

## Business Problem

The hotel currently manages guest enquiries, corporate booking requests, event enquiries, and follow-up activities through disconnected spreadsheets, emails, and manual processes.

This makes it difficult to:

- Track guest enquiries
- Follow up on booking opportunities
- Manage corporate clients
- Monitor event booking requests
- Assign tasks to staff
- Track customer communication
- Measure sales performance
- Generate accurate reports

## Proposed Solution

Zoho CRM will be configured to provide one central system for managing guest relationships, booking opportunities, corporate accounts, event enquiries, follow-up activities, workflow automation, and reporting.

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how Zoho CRM can support the sales and customer relationship processes of a luxury hospitality business.
