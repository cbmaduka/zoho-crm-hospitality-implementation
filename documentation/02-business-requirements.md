# Business Requirements Document

## Client

LuxeStay Hotels

## Industry

Hospitality and Hotel Management

## Project Objective

Implement Zoho CRM to centralize customer relationship management, improve sales processes, automate follow-up activities, and provide management with real-time reporting.

---

# Current Challenges

The business currently experiences the following challenges:

- Guest enquiries are tracked manually through emails and spreadsheets.
- Corporate booking requests are difficult to monitor.
- Sales follow-ups are inconsistent.
- Event booking opportunities are not centrally managed.
- Customer communication history is fragmented.
- Management lacks visibility into sales performance.
- Reporting is manual and time-consuming.

---

# Business Requirements

The CRM system should enable the business to:

- Capture guest enquiries.
- Manage individual and corporate customers.
- Track booking opportunities from enquiry to confirmation.
- Record customer interactions.
- Schedule follow-up activities.
- Automate reminders and notifications.
- Generate sales and performance reports.
- Improve customer relationship management.

---

# Expected Benefits

Following implementation, the business expects to achieve:

- Improved enquiry management.
- Faster response times.
- Better visibility into the sales pipeline.
- Increased booking conversion rates.
- Improved customer satisfaction.
- Reduced manual administrative work.
- Centralized customer information.
- Better decision-making through reporting.
