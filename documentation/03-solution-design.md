# Solution Design

## Project

LuxeStay Hotels Zoho CRM Implementation

---

# Solution Overview

Zoho CRM will be configured to centralize customer information, manage booking opportunities, improve customer engagement, automate repetitive tasks, and provide management with real-time business insights.

The implementation will support both individual guests and corporate customers while improving operational efficiency across the reservations and sales teams.

---

# Functional Solution

The solution will include the following CRM components:

- Leads
- Contacts
- Accounts
- Deals
- Activities
- Tasks
- Calls
- Meetings
- Reports
- Dashboards
- Workflow Automation
- Email Templates

# Automation Strategy

The CRM will automate:

- Lead assignment
- Follow-up reminders
- Email confirmations
- Activity notifications
- Deal stage updates
- Task creation
- Customer follow-up emails

---

# Reporting Strategy

Management dashboards will provide visibility into:

- New enquiries
- Active bookings
- Booking conversion rates
- Corporate partnerships
- Sales pipeline
- Team activities
- Revenue opportunities

---

# Security Strategy

Access will be controlled using user roles and profiles to ensure employees only access information relevant to their responsibilities.

---

# Expected Business Outcome

The proposed solution will improve customer relationship management, increase operational efficiency, standardize sales activities, and provide accurate reporting to support management decision-making.
