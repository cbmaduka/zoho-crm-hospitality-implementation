# Validation Rules

## Overview

Validation Rules were implemented to improve reservation data quality by ensuring critical information is entered before a reservation can be saved.

---

## Configured Validation Rules

### Check-in Date

Purpose

- Prevent invalid reservation dates.

Business Value

- Ensures reservation dates are entered correctly before processing.

---

### Number of Guests

Purpose

- Requires at least one guest for every reservation.

Business Value

- Prevents incomplete reservation records.

---

### Booking Type(s)

Purpose

- Requires every reservation to be categorized by booking type.

Business Value

- Supports reservation routing, reporting, and workflow automation.

---

### Corporate Rate Requested

Purpose

- Validates corporate booking information before submission.

Business Value

- Ensures corporate reservations include pricing information required for approval and processing.

---

## Implementation Outcome

The validation rules improve data accuracy, reduce manual correction, and ensure reservation records contain the minimum information required for hotel operations.
